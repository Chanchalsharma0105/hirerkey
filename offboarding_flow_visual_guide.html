📌 Executive Lifecycle Overview
Every employee departure in Hirerkey follows an auditable 6-stage operational pipeline: (1) Real-World Event / Email → (2) HR Initiates in Screen 1 (+ Start Modal) → (3) Operational Handover & Notice Tracking → (4) 4-Way Departmental Clearance (IT, Admin, Finance, Line Manager) → (5) Exit Interview & Statutory Settlement (AED) → (6) Case Closed & Profile Archived to Employees › Exit.
