Modal & Drawer Standardization Decision Matrix

Evaluate all paradigms side-by-side: the 5 legacy overlays vs. the 2 proposed revamp standards.

Paradigm Type Visual Architecture Header Treatment Opening Animation Best-Fit SaaS Use Case Standardization Verdict
🚀 REVAMP: Fluid Edge Side Sheet Flush edge (p: 0), 100vh full height, width min(1120px, 94vw) Top gradient stripe + clean white header with breadcrumbs Spring Deceleration (320ms) + Canvas depth scale Complex audits, Expense vouchers, AI Studio, multi-file inspection ★ OFFICIAL SIDE STANDARD (M2-inspired)
💎 REVAMP: Spring Pop Center Dialog Centered (520px max), 20px radius, inner glass stroke Clean card header with Hirerkey purple accent stripe Scale 0.92 ➔ 1.015 ➔ 1.0 (280ms) + Frosted blur (8px) Quick creations, role elevations, confirmations, 1-4 field forms ★ OFFICIAL CENTER STANDARD (Zero side drift)
🟣 Legacy M1: Purple Right Drawer Right anchor, inset margin (p: 2.5), floating card with shadow Solid Purple (#7C3AED), white text Native right slide (translateX: 0) Inspection views, HR Broadcasts, filters, profile drawers Retain as alternative drawer variant
🔲 Legacy M2: Full-Bleed Sheet Flush edge (p: 0), 100vh height, wide canvas (min 1140px, 94vw) Integrated canvas header with tabs & status Flush horizontal slide Heavy multi-column workflows, Expense auditing, document extractors Precursor to the Revamped Side Sheet
⚪ Legacy M3: Neutral White Drawer Right anchor, inset margin (p: 3), 480px width Clean white surface, dark text Standard right slide Offboarding clearance, ticket filters, succession planning Replace with Revamped Side Sheet
📐 Legacy M4: Orientation Center Sheet 50vw width, 92vh height, centered (top/left: 50%) White header with purple title @keyframes IO: slides from +20% right into center Heavy creation wizards (Orientation, Tour, Checklists) Replace with Revamped Side Sheet
🪟 Legacy M5: Theme Center Dialog 480px width, centered dialog with backdrop Subtle card header @keyframes htsModalSlideInRight (translate -30% to -50%) Quick adds (Department, Grade), confirmations, deletes Replace with Revamped Spring Pop Dialog

💡 Design System Architect Recommendation

By standardizing on these two proposed standards (Revamped Side Sheet for all inspection/multi-step flows and Revamped Center Dialog for quick actions/confirmations), Hirerkey eliminates the disorienting horizontal drift on center dialogs and unifies all 61 modules under an effortless, world-class interaction model.
